title: SLA-by-severity policy

Our service-level agreement sets both a first-response time and a resolution target for each priority. P1 tickets get a first response within 15 minutes and a resolution target of 4 hours, and P2 tickets get a response within 1 hour and resolution within 24 hours. P3 tickets get a response within 4 hours and resolution within 72 hours, and P4 tickets get a response within 24 hours and resolution within 7 days. The SLA clock pauses only while we are waiting for information that the customer has been asked to provide.
