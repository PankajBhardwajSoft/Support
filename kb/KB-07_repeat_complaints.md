title: Repeat-complaint handling policy

A complaint counts as a repeat when the same customer raises the same issue three or more times within 30 days. Repeat complaints are raised one priority level and assigned to a single named agent who owns them until closure. That agent must look for the root cause, not just close the latest ticket, and must record what was done to stop it happening again. Customers with a repeat complaint get a call-back offer rather than chat-only follow-up.
