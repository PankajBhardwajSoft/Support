title: Ticket priority classification rules

Every new support ticket is given one of four priority levels: P1 Critical, P2 High, P3 Medium or P4 Low. A ticket is P1 when a rider or driver is unsafe, stranded, or locked out mid-trip, and P2 when money has been charged wrongly or a driver cannot go online. Ride-quality complaints and app glitches that have a workaround are P3, while general questions and suggestions are P4. Agents may raise a ticket's priority at any time but may lower it only with a team lead's approval.
