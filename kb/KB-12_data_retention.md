title: Data retention policy for tickets

Support tickets and their chat history are kept for 24 months after the ticket is closed, and are then deleted. Tickets linked to a safety incident, legal claim or payment dispute are kept for 7 years. Recorded support calls are deleted after 90 days unless they are attached to one of those cases. Customers can ask for a copy of their ticket data, and the request must be fulfilled within 30 days.
