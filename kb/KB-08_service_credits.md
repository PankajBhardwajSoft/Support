title: Service-credit policy

Service credits are non-cash credits added to a customer's Ola wallet to make up for a service failure. A rider gets a credit of 10 percent of the fare when a driver arrives more than 15 minutes after the confirmed pickup time. Credits can also be given when we miss a P1 or P2 SLA target, up to a limit of 500 rupees per ticket. Service credits expire after 90 days and cannot be withdrawn as cash.
