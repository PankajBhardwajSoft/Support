title: Feedback collection process

After a ticket is marked Resolved, the customer receives a short in-app survey asking them to rate the support from 1 to 5 and add an optional comment. The survey stays open for 7 days and is sent only once per ticket. Any rating of 2 or lower automatically reopens a review task for a team lead. Feedback themes are summarised every month and shared with product and operations teams.
