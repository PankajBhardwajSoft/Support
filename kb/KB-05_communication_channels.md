title: Customer communication channel policy

Customers can reach support through in-app chat, the help centre email form, or the support phone line. In-app chat is the default channel and must be used for any update on an open ticket, so the full history stays in one place. The phone line is reserved for P1 safety and stranded-trip issues. Agents must never ask a customer to share a password, OTP or full card number on any channel.
