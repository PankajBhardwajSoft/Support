title: Outage communication protocol

When an outage affects booking, payments or driver login in any city, the incident lead posts an in-app banner within 15 minutes of confirming it. Status updates are posted at least every 30 minutes until the service is restored. Support agents link all related tickets to the incident instead of answering each one separately. Within 48 hours of recovery, affected customers receive a summary of what happened and any service credit they are owed.
