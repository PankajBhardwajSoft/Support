title: Escalation matrix

A ticket moves to Tier 2 support when it breaches 75 percent of its SLA resolution target or when the frontline agent cannot resolve it. Tier 2 hands the ticket to the city operations manager if it is still open after a further 24 hours, or immediately if it involves rider or driver safety. Safety incidents and any legal threat are escalated straight to the Trust and Safety team, whatever the ticket's age. Every escalation must record the reason and the name of the person who now owns the ticket.
