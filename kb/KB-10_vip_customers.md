title: VIP customer handling policy

VIP customers are corporate account users and riders in the Ola loyalty top tier. Their tickets start one priority level higher than normal, but never above P1. VIP tickets are routed to a dedicated support queue staffed by senior agents, and a named account manager is told about any P1 or P2 issue. VIP status never overrides the safety rules or the refund rules that apply to everyone.
