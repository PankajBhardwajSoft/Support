title: Refund and compensation policy

Riders get a full refund when a trip is charged but never completed, or when they are billed twice for the same ride. If the fare is more than 20 percent above the estimate shown at booking with no change of route, the difference is refunded to the original payment method. Refunds to cards and UPI usually arrive within 5 to 7 working days, while refunds to the Ola wallet arrive within 24 hours. Compensation beyond the fare, such as a ride voucher, needs a team lead's approval.
