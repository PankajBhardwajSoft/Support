title: Business-hours and holiday support policy

In-app chat and the P1 phone line are staffed 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Email support and Tier 2 teams work from 8 AM to 10 PM local time, Monday to Saturday. On national holidays, email and Tier 2 replies may take one extra business day, but P1 and P2 SLA targets do not change. The holiday calendar is published in the help centre at the start of each year.
